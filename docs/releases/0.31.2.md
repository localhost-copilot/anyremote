## Fixed in 0.31.2

- **Only your windows come over.** A new window on the other Mac now opens here only when its app is working for you: the app you last clicked, typed or chose a menu command in, one whose window you opened, or an app it launched (a document double-clicked in its Finder, a link in its Mail). Whoever acts on an app last has it, so a window opened by a script, a login item or someone at the other Mac stays there, even right after your click. You can still open it from the picker.
- **Apps launched on the other Mac open on its own screen.** An app started from that Mac's Dock or by a script could open its window on the virtual display that holds your remote windows, where nobody at that Mac could see it; such windows now move to its main screen.
- **The other Mac's microphone setting comes back.** After a session with your microphone, the AnyRemote Microphone could stay that Mac's default input for good. It now hands back the earlier input, or a built-in microphone or another input when the earlier one is gone or unknown.

One limit remains: macOS doesn't tell who asks an app that is already running to open something. A script opening a document in the app you are using brings that window over, and a link you click in Mail opens on the other Mac when its user used the browser last.

## Install

Requires macOS 15 or later (universal: Apple silicon and Intel).

AnyRemote 0.26.0 and later offer this update by themselves, or use AnyRemote › Check for Updates…. To install by hand, unzip `AnyRemote-0.31.2.zip` into `/Applications`. Then clear the quarantine flag once:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AnyRemote.app
```

0.31.2 uses protocol v24, like 0.31.0, so it works with Macs still on that. The fixes are on the Mac being controlled: update that one.
