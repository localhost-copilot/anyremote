## Fixed in 0.29.2

- **Only the windows you open come over.** While you use a remote window, a window that opens on the other Mac comes to this Mac when your click or key led to it: a new window of the app you're using; an app or document you double-click in its Finder, or a link that starts another app; or an app you bring forward from it, such as Finder revealing a file or a browser opening a link. Apps that someone opens on the other Mac itself, apps that start by themselves, and windows that appear in the background stay there; open them from the window list when you want them.
- 0.29.1 kept the windows of apps that a double-click in Finder started on the other Mac; they come over again.

## Also in 0.29

- **Higher frame rates.** Video Quality has a Frame Rate setting for each Mac you control: 60 (the default), 90, 120 or 144 Hz, for smoother motion on fast screens such as a MacBook Pro's.

## Install

Requires macOS 15 or later (universal: Apple silicon and Intel).

AnyRemote 0.26.0 and later offer this update by themselves, or use AnyRemote › Check for Updates…. To install by hand, unzip `AnyRemote-0.29.2.zip` into `/Applications`. Then clear the quarantine flag once:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AnyRemote.app
```

0.29.2 uses protocol v23, like 0.29.0 and 0.29.1, so it can't connect to 0.28.x. Update every Mac: once this Mac runs 0.29.2, it offers to update the other Macs it is paired with when it connects to them.
