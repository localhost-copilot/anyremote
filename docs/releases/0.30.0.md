## New in 0.30.0

- **Apps looks like macOS's Apps view.** Larger icons in an even grid: a row of the apps you opened lately, then every app by name, with a dot under the running ones. Chips above it show one category at a time, such as Developer Tools or Utilities (the other Mac needs 0.30.0 to send the categories).
- **All Windows.** The switch at the top right of Windows shows every window at its own shape, side by side as in Mission Control, besides the list grouped by app. Thumbnails move to their new places when you switch.
- **Search filters as you type,** also while a Chinese input method is still composing, and finds Chinese names by their pinyin or its initials (`jsq` finds 计算器). ↑ and ↓ in the search field move the selection; Return opens it.
- **Animations.** Windows and Apps slide when you switch between them, and search results move into place. Window cards tilt toward the pointer and dip when clicked; app icons lift and dip.

## Fixed

- Double-clicking an app in Recent no longer opens the wrong one: the row keeps its order until the picker is hidden instead of moving the app you just opened to the front between the two clicks.

## Install

Requires macOS 15 or later (universal: Apple silicon and Intel).

AnyRemote 0.26.0 and later offer this update by themselves, or use AnyRemote › Check for Updates…. To install by hand, unzip `AnyRemote-0.30.0.zip` into `/Applications`. Then clear the quarantine flag once:

```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AnyRemote.app
```

0.30.0 uses protocol v23, like 0.29.x, so it works with Macs still on 0.29; app categories appear once the other Mac runs 0.30.0 too.
